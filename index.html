A&S Painting and Plastering Inc

About

A&S Painting and Plastering Inc provides full-service painting wall coverings, waterproofing and roof prep, and surface preparation for residential and commercial projects. We offer free estimates in person or via digital blueprints and work with builders, contractors, HOA and property managers across the Bay Area, Monterey Bay Peninsula, and Central Valley. Our crews follow OSHA safety standards on every job.

  • Full-service painting and new paint projects
  • Wall covering installation
  • Waterproofing and roof prep
  • Surface preparation and drywall repair
  • Digital blueprint estimating and on-site assessments
  • Trusted project scheduling and reliable timelines
  • Color and finish consultation
  • Clean, safe jobsite management

What You Can Expect

Reliable painting with careful prep, clear communication, budget-conscious planning that helps keep projects within budget, and a clean finish that protects your property and looks great for years.
