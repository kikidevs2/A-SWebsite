About & Capabilities

A&S Painting provides waterproofing, roof coating, wall covering, repainting, and surface repair services for residential, commercial, and builder partner projects. We offer free estimates in person or via digital blueprints and work with HOAs, property managers, and builders across the Bay Area, Monterey Bay Peninsula, and Central Valley. Our crews follow OSHA safety standards on every job.

  • Waterproofing, roof coatings, wall coverings, and repainting
  • Free estimates in person or via digital blueprints
  • Surface preparation, drywall repair, and substrate repair
  • Digital blueprint estimating and on-site assessments
  • Licensed and insured through Acisure West Region, with safety-first workmanship
  • OSHA safety standards compliance on every project
  • More than 40 years serving the region
